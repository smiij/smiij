### Pacific Bonsai Museum Image Uploader
Summary: This project was begun with the hope of making the process of uploading photos/organizing files for the Pacific Bonsai Museum staff more efficient by cutting down on search for different images. Coming up with a UI that was simple but clean, and a tagging process that was intuitive and relevant took time but as development in our chosen solution, the Batch Image Tagger/Uploader, has progressed and usability testing has been done, we realize from feedback that we've been able to make a tool that the museum staff would actually use in their day-to-day, which was always the goal. As we continue developing our solution, making the upload process on our backend smoother, and making sure that our app connects to the staff's Sharepoint and working with the museum's IT staff to ensure they can host this in their tenant without issue is our next focus. As a team we've been able to develop something using our cumulative experiences in the last four years, and as for myself personally, I've been able to prove to myself that I could have a hand in developing something impactful, which has improved my confidence and changed my perspective as I come to the end of my college experience.
